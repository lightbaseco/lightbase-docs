title: "Data curation"
description: "Make Lightbase speak the same language as your company."

After [indexing](/architecture/indexing-process), Lightbase holds an inferred model of your code, your infrastructure, and your data. With a full understanding of the business concepts, connections and dependencies of the different flows of your system. Data curation is the tool for manually editing that model, so it matches the way your company groups and names internal business concepts, for the kind of knowledge that is not explicitly represented in the code but is of common use inside your team, like certain domain boundaries, internal business wording or custom grouping.

## What happens when your code changes

Manual edits take precedence over inference, so a later analysis never overwrites what you curate.

When new code arrives — a new model, a new service, a new external call — Lightbase automatically integrates it into the existent model and flags
it for review, so a human always has the last word on what the model says about your system.

<Info>
  Everything you curate is internally marked, so your semantics are always distinguishable from what Lightbase inferred.
</Info>

Real production codebases can span hundreds or thousands of services, with relationships between them that are not always clear. A change in one service can easily have unintended consequences in downstream consumers, especially when they belong to other teams. This increases the risk of production incidents or subtle data corruption that could be harder to detect.

Lightbase is the code intelligence layer for large codebases. It indexes your repositories, traces each code flow from the entrypoint that starts it, and builds one model of your system:

- **Every service and endpoint**, and the calls that cross between them.
- **The databases, queues and external providers** each flow reaches.
- **The schema of every database**, reconstructed from the code that defines and queries it.
- **The lineage of the data** that moves through the system.

While planning a feature or a refactor, you see the internal and third-party services the work involves, the business flows a change will impact, and the lineage of every column it touches.

Through the MCP server, your agents reach the same model, so they can verify the impact of a change on downstream services and not introduce hard to catch bugs or silent data corruption.

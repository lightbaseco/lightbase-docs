A **counterparty group** represents an external entity your company interacts with. Groups can be:

- **Technical** — for example, AWS.
- **Business** — for example, providers, contracted SaaS, or clients.

A single counterparty can belong to multiple groups. Describe how to create, edit, and organize counterparties and their groups.

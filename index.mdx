Lightbase connects to your source control, indexes your repositories, and builds a map of what your software actually does. Instead of stopping at the boundary of a single file or service, Lightbase follows your business flows from their entrypoints all the way to the databases, queues, and external services they touch.

## Why Lightbase is different

Unlike static analysis tools that reason about one service in isolation, Lightbase traces business flows **end-to-end**, even when a single flow spans multiple services through network calls, message queues, and other asynchronous boundaries. This lets Lightbase link external service calls and database access back to the concrete business flows that trigger them, giving you a picture of your system that matches how it really behaves in production.

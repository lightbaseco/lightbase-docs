Lightbase connects to your source control, indexes the repositories you choose, and builds a model of how your system works. This page walks you through connecting a provider, letting Lightbase build that model, and querying it from your AI client.

## Before you start

You need:

- Admin access in your Lightbase organization
- Permission to authorize or install integrations in your source control provider
- Access to the repositories, group, or workspace you want Lightbase to use

## Set up Lightbase

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your source control provider">
    Connect Lightbase to GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket. This authorizes Lightbase to read the repositories you choose. See [Source providers](/getting-started/source-providers) for the per-provider steps and requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the repositories to index">
    During the connection, you choose which repositories Lightbase indexes. The rest stay disabled and are excluded from analysis. You can change this selection at any time. See [Repository management](/getting-started/repositories) to edit it later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lightbase builds your model">
    Lightbase indexes the selected repositories automatically to build the first version of your organization's model. You don't need to trigger anything. See [the indexing process](/architecture/indexing-process) for what happens during the initial index.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the MCP server">
    Give your AI client access to your model through the Lightbase MCP server. Point your client at:

    ```
    https://mcp.lightbase.co
    ```

    The server authenticates the call directly, with no token to generate or pass. The first connection opens a browser window to sign in with your Lightbase account. See [MCP server](/mcp/overview) for per-client setup, including VS Code, Cursor, and Claude Code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Curate your data (optional)">
    Data curation aligns the inferred model with the names and structure your team uses. See [Data curation](/data-curation/overview) to learn what you can refine.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Real production codebases can span hundreds or thousands of services, with relationships between them that are not always clear. A change in one service can easily have unintended consequences in downstream consumers, especially when they belong to other teams. This increases the risk of production incidents or subtle data corruption that could be harder to detect.

Lightbase is the code intelligence layer for large codebases, built on a model of every service, endpoint, call between services, and the data they share. It indexes your repositories and traces each code flow from the entrypoint that starts it to the databases, queues, and external providers it reaches.

You explore that model while designing new functionality, and your agents reach the same model through the MCP server.

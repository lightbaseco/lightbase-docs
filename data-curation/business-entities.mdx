---
title: "Business entities"
description: "Group tables that are part of the same business concept."
---

A **business entity** defines a single business concept that is distributed through multiple tables. `Users` is an entity: the users table plus every normalized table hanging off it — profiles, credentials, addresses, preferences, consents. Your domain has one word for all of that, and a business entity is where that word lives.

Entities are not limited to one database or one service. When the same concept is stored by several applications, a single entity covers all of it, so the concept answers as one thing no matter how many places it is written to.

## Lightbase proposes, your team decides

Lightbase generates a first set of entities from your code and your curated schema. That set is a proposal by design: entity vocabulary is the most company-specific thing in any system. One organization's *customer* is another's *account*, and a third calls it a *party* because that is the word its regulator uses.

<Tip>
  Start with the ten or twenty entities that carry your business. Coverage matters less than getting the core vocabulary right — the long tail can wait until someone needs it.
</Tip>

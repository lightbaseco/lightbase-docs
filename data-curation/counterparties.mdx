A [counterparty](/core-concepts/counterparties) is an external technical component your code interacts with, such as an S3 bucket or the Stripe API. How Indexer groups and names them comes from the business context it extracts from the code, and will not always match how your company refers to them, or its view of what counts as a single counterparty. **Counterparties** allows you to make the necessary edits to the model.

## Counterparty groups

A [counterparty group](/core-concepts/counterparties#how-counterparties-are-grouped) collects counterparties that belong together. Groups come in two kinds, technical and business, and a counterparty can belong to several at once. A bucket holding a customer's data sits in the S3 technical group and in that customer's business group.

Technical groups are inferred from the provider, such as Stripe or S3. Business groups are inferred from the intent observed in the code, which is harder to detect reliably, so they are the ones that usually need curating:

* **Create a business group** for a supplier, a customer or a partner.
* **Assign counterparties** to the groups they belong to.
* **Move a counterparty** between groups when a relationship changes.

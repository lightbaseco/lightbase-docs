---
title: "Applications"
description: "Manage the applications detected in your code and group them by team or domain."
---

An **application** is a deployable unit from your codebase: a backend service, a frontend web app, or a set of scripts.

[Detection](/architecture/indexing-process) names each application after its repository or manifest, which is often not the name your team uses. In curation you can rename them and group them by team, domain, stack or any custom grouping you require.

## Application groups

Group applications into the units you work with: a product, a domain, a platform, a team's surface area. Lightbase then rolls flows and impact up to that level. A blast radius reports "three applications in Payments" instead of listing the parent mono-repo.

## Environment variables

Applications reach their databases and their peers through configuration. Supply the values behind those variables per environment. Lightbase then resolves which [curated database](/data-curation/databases) or application a `DATABASE_URL` points at, turning an inferred connection into a confirmed one.

## Enable and disable

Disable an application to take it out of the model without changing what you index. Use it for a deprecated application, a folder of generated code, or a false positive from application detection.

<Note>
  You can always reenable the application to start indexing it again.
</Note>

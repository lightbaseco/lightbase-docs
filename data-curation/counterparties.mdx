A [counterparty](/core-concepts/counterparties) is something outside your system that your code interacts with: a cloud service, a payment provider, a partner API, a SaaS tool you pay for. Lightbase detects them as it traces your flows and records what kind of interaction it found. In **Counterparties** you rename them, describe what each interaction is for, and organize them into the groups your company uses.

Names and groups are inferred. In curation you can enhance them: rename a counterparty to fit the way your team calls it internally, merge variants of the same one, or change the groups it belongs to.

## Counterparty groups

A [counterparty group](/core-concepts/counterparties#how-counterparties-are-grouped) collects counterparties that belong together. Groups come in two kinds.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Technical" icon="cloud">
    Infrastructure and platforms, for example `AWS`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Business" icon="briefcase">
    Suppliers, contracted SaaS, clients, or any commercial relationship.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

A counterparty can belong to several groups at once. A payments endpoint sits in the group for that vendor and in the group you use to track everything that processes cardholder data.

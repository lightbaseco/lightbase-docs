A **counterparty** is something outside your system that your code interacts with: a cloud service, a payment provider, a partner API, a SaaS tool you pay for. Lightbase detects them as it traces your flows and records what kind of interaction it found. In **Counterparties** you rename them, describe what each interaction is for, and organize them into the groups your company uses.

Names and groups are inferred. In curation you correct them: rename a counterparty, merge variants of the same one, and change the groups it belongs to.

## Counterparty groups

A **counterparty group** represents one external entity your company deals with. Groups come in two kinds.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Technical" icon="cloud">
    Infrastructure and platforms, for example `AWS`.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Business" icon="briefcase">
    Suppliers, contracted SaaS, clients, or any commercial relationship.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

A counterparty can belong to several groups at once. A payments endpoint sits in the group for that vendor and in the group you use to track everything that processes cardholder data.

## What you can edit

| Object             | You curate                                       | Lightbase provides                                |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Counterparty       | Name, description, group membership              | The interaction type found in code                |
| Counterparty group | Name, kind, and which counterparties belong to it | The initial groups, inferred from calls and flows |

**Applications** are every service, frontend application, set of scripts... from your codebase. 

[Detection](/architecture/application-detection) takes its names from repositories and manifests, which might be different from the way you internally call it. Through curation you can also group them by team, domain, technical stack...

## Application groups

Group applications into the units you plan work around: a product, a domain, a platform, a team's surface area. Grouping lets Lightbase roll flows and impact up to that level, so a blast radius reports "three services in Payments" instead of listing nine repositories.

## Environment variables

Applications reach their databases and their peers through configuration, not hardcoded addresses. Supply the values behind those variables per environment and Lightbase resolves which [curated database](/data-curation/databases) or service endpoint a `DATABASE_URL` really points at. That is what turns an inferred connection into a confirmed one.

## Enable and disable

Disable an application to take it out of the model without changing what you index. It is the right move for a deprecated service, a folder of generated code, or a false positive from application detection.

<Note>
  You can always reenable the application to start indexing it again.
</Note>
